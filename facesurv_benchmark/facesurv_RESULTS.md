# FaceSurv — Phase-1 Face Identification Benchmark

Model: InsightFace **buffalo_l / ArcFace** (512-d), the same embedder used by the Phase-1 pipeline (`step5_6_embed.py`). Match = cosine similarity vs. per-subject gallery templates.

## Setup
- Gallery (enrolled subjects): **228** (mean of each subject's portraits)
- Probe tracks: **720** total (688 enrolled / 32 open-set distractors)
- Probe faces embedded: **6174** (face-detection rate 85.8%; 1026 crops had no detectable face)
- Frames sampled per track: up to 10; sessions: day,night

## Identification accuracy (closed-set, enrolled subjects only)

| Metric | Frame-level | Track-level |
|---|---|---|
| Rank-1 accuracy | 91.94% | 98.40% |
| Rank-5 accuracy | 96.14% | 99.13% |
| Rank-1 @ threshold (≥0.4) | 67.30% | 86.19% |
| N (probe items) | 5955 | 688 |

Rank-1 counts a probe correct when the true subject is its nearest gallery template (no threshold). Rank-1 @ threshold additionally requires that top similarity to clear the pipeline's operating point (cosine ≥ 0.4) — i.e. what the live pipeline would actually accept.

## Verification metrics (genuine vs. impostor cosine scores)

- ROC-AUC: **0.9856**
- EER: **4.70%**
- TAR @ FAR=1%: **90.85%**
- TAR @ FAR=0.1%: **82.60%**
- Mean cosine — genuine 0.494 vs. impostor 0.0396
- Pairs: 5955 genuine / 29775 impostor

## Open-set separability
- Mean top-1 cosine for **enrolled** probes: 0.5028
- Mean top-1 cosine for **distractor** probes: 0.2495
